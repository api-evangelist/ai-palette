# AI Palette

> AI Palette is an AI-first consumer-insights and product-innovation platform for the Consumer Packaged Goods (CPG) industry, aggregating 61 billion data points across 24 countries and 18 languages to help food, beverage, and personal-care brands discover emerging trends, generate and screen new product concepts, and accelerate launches. Founded in Singapore in 2018; acquired by GlobalData. This is an enterprise SaaS platform accessed by license — there is no public developer API, SDK, or specification.

## Products
- [Foresight Engine](https://www.aipalette.com/platform/): Real-time trend discovery across e-commerce, menus, recipes, social media, and search in 18 languages / 24 countries.
- [Concept Genie](https://www.aipalette.com/platform/): Generative-AI creation of new product and marketing concepts from Foresight Engine data.
- [Screen Winner](https://www.aipalette.com/platform/): Virtual validation and screening of product concepts for a category or market.
- [FoodGPT / Arya](https://www.aipalette.com/platform/): An F&B-trained agentic AI co-pilot for the end-to-end innovation workflow, supporting integration of a customer's own data sources.
